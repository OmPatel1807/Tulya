# Tulya - Official Document Sources and Provenance

This document records the provenance, exact retrieval endpoints, file metadata, and official usage policies for all primary source legal documents in `data/raw/`.

## Official Repository Information
- **Primary Source Portal**: [India Code Portal (indiacode.gov.in)](https://indiacode.gov.in)
- **Publisher**: Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India
- **Repository Architecture**: DSpace REST API / Institutional Digital Repository

## Website Terms of Use & Copyright Policy Summary
According to the official Copyright Policy published by the Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice at `https://indiacode.gov.in/info/copyright-policy`:
- **Permitted Uses**:
  - Educational, academic research, and personal study.
  - Legal reference, research, and citation in court proceedings.
  - Viewing and downloading copies for non-commercial reference.
  - Mandatory citation format: *"IndiaCode Portal, Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India."*
- **Restrictions & Flags**:
  - **Non-Commercial**: Commercial sale, sublicensing, or monetization of raw gazette text is prohibited without authorization.
  - **Bulk Scraping & Automated Redistribution**: Unauthorized automated harvesting or bulk redistribution is prohibited; automated access must respect rate limits and polite crawling etiquette.
  - **Integrity**: Contents must not be reproduced in a misleading or derogatory manner.
