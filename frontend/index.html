Constitutional Framework

Two Sheets. One Law.

Under Article 348 of the Constitution and the Official Languages Act, Indian Central Acts are published in both English and Hindi. Citizens and courts reference both texts.

Differences between official language versions can change how a law is read.

Semantic Review

Discretion vs. Duty

Modal auxiliaries define executive powers. A divergence between discretionary phrasing ("may") and mandatory phrasing ("shall" / करेगा) shifts statutory authority.

English Discretionary Phrasing
"The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions..."
Hindi Mandatory Phrasing (हिन्दी)
"केन्द्रीय सरकार उपबंधों को कार्यान्वित करने के लिए नियम बनाएगी..."
