Constitutional Foundation

Two Languages. Equal Legal Authority.

Under Article 348 of the Constitution and the Official Languages Act, Indian Central Statutes are enacted and published in both English and Hindi. Citizens and courts are entitled to rely on either official gazette.

Modality Shifts

From Discretion to Mandatory Duty

A single modal verb shifts the balance of executive power. When an English "may" is rendered as "करेगा" (shall) rather than "सकेगा" (may), discretionary authority turns into an absolute statutory mandate.

English Discretionary Power
"The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions..."
Hindi Mandatory Obligation (हिन्दी)
"केन्द्रीय सरकार उपबंधों को कार्यान्वित करने के लिए नियम बनाएगी..."
